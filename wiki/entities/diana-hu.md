## Playbook AI-native (2026)

Ver detalhes completos em [[y-combinator]] e [[2026-05-28_founded-ceo-yc-startup-ia]].

Os 6 princípios capturados no carousel:
- AI as Operating System
- Closed loops everywhere
- No more human middleware
- Three employee archetypes (IC/builder-operator, DRI, AI founder)
- Token-max, not headcount-max
- Early-stage advantage
